About

Who We Are

The Problem We Solve

Most AI security tools rely on closed, cloud-hosted LLMs that cannot process sensitive client data. Security researchers working on enterprise engagements need the intelligence of frontier models — but without the data sovereignty risk that comes from sending client environments to a third-party API.

Our Approach

Apex Solutions was founded in January 2026 to close that gap. We build on open-source models — DeepSeek V4 Flash and Qwen3-35B-A3B — deployed on customer-controlled GPU infrastructure. Security teams get frontier-class intelligence without the compliance risk.

Our cloud inference platform uses SkyPilot to orchestrate GPU workloads across AWS, GCP, and Azure — automatically selecting preemptible instances to minimize cost. The same GGUF models run identically on local RTX hardware or a cloud A100-80GB, making development and production environments truly portable.

Where We Are Now

We are bootstrapped, generating revenue from security research engagements, and iterating toward a broader platform. Our products are in active use for enterprise red-team work, with 75–99% of our infrastructure spend on AWS. We are a 1–10 person team based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India, operating since January 2026.
